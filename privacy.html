Privacy Policy

What we collect

Clipping Publisher receives the minimum account identifiers and authorization tokens returned when the operator connects a social account. It also records publishing status, public post identifiers, campaign identifiers, and public performance metrics needed to operate and audit the workflow.

How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate the operator, upload authorized content, monitor publication status, retrieve public analytics, and maintain operational records. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Credentials are stored in the operator's protected system credential store. Data is shared only with the connected platforms when needed to perform an operator-requested action, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

The operator can revoke platform access and delete stored credentials from the protected system credential store. Operational records are retained only as long as needed for campaign accounting and compliance. Requests for access or deletion may be sent to toby.onabolu@gmail.com.

Security and changes

We use least-privilege platform scopes and protect stored credentials from source control and public logs. Material policy changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
