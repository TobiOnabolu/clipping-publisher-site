Authorized content workflow

From long streams to focused short-form clips.

Clipping Publisher lets its operator initiate and control an automated workflow that detects authorized livestream moments, prepares vertical edits, and publishes approved content to connected social accounts.

1. Source

Only campaign-approved channels and recordings enter the workflow.

2. Prepare

Candidate moments are edited, captioned, and checked against campaign rules.

3. Publish

The account owner authorizes each connected platform through its official API.

Clipping Publisher is an independent operator tool. It is not affiliated with TikTok, Twitch, Kick, YouTube, or campaign platforms.
